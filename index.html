What Is SmartBox
What SmartBox Is

SmartBox is essentially an infrastructure-in-a-box for the manufacturing floor: a replicable standard unit that packages edge computing hardware, industrial connectivity, and an extensible intelligent application stack together.

Connect the Floor Bridge PLCs, sensors, equipment control layers, and upper-level data platforms to continuously collect high-resolution process data.
Compute at the Edge Run analytics, visualization, and machine learning models close to the production line so judgment and response stay close to the process.
Close the Loop Go beyond watching data by feeding results back to operators and control systems for warning, adjustment, and automatic closed-loop control.
This architecture shows SmartBox centered on the ControlEdge Technology Stack, connecting upward to data, AI, vision, and applications, and downward to edge computing, control, and shopfloor devices.
